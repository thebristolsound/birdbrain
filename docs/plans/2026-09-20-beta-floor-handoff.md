# Beta floor handoff: the sixteen disputed beta-blocking rows

The defect review of 2026-09-19 (`docs/plans/2026-09-19-defect-review.md`) placed 18 open
bugs in the beta-blocking bucket. A second agent disputed 16 of the 17 listed rows, and in
every case the dispute has the same shape: the code fact is true, and the refuter argues a
round-1 tester does not reach it. Whether a path is on the beta floor is the maintainer's
call, so this page hands those 16 rows over with the two positions side by side.

#1270 was the one undisputed row and carries the `queued` label as of 2026-09-20. The ten
undisputed fixed-on-main rows were closed the same day.

## How to answer

Write `block` or `later` in the Decision column. `block` means the issue gets `queued` and
joins the pre-tag fix list. `later` means it keeps its current labels and waits. A row can
also take `info` when neither position is safe without a repro on the tagged build.
