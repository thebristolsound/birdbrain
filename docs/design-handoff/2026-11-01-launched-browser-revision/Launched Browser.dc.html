My answer to question 1: the rail gives way, not the Captures screen.i
Where the 320-pixel rail does not fit, it folds to a 40-pixel strip with no separator, exactly as the Captures screen’s side columns fold. The strip holds an expand control, the REC dot, the Capture button, the tab count, and the last Exhibit Number. Expanding opens the full panel as an overlay over the viewer, 320 pixels wide, which closes on Escape or on a click outside; it does not reflow the screen. The pop-out stays available from the overlay’s header.
Build change I ask for: make the details panel’s fold read the room left beside the rail, not the window’s width. Then from 1,100 to about 1,300 the details panel folds and the rail docks, and the strip is needed only below about 1,020.i
