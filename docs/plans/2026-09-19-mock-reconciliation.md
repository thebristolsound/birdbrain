## How to use this

The direction column is the maintainer's product call and no agent fills it. Write mock, app or drop in it: mock means the app moves to the design, app means the design is superseded and the mock is the stale side, drop means neither and the row is closed without work. A mock-ahead row that already carries a related issue attaches to that issue once the direction is mock. A row with no issue gets one only after the direction is chosen, so that the issue can state the decision rather than reopen the comparison.
