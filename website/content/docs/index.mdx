title: "Introduction"
description: "Local-first web evidence capture for OSINT investigations — hashed, timestamped, and chained into a verifiable audit manifest."
---

Birdbrain is an open-source desktop app with a companion Chromium extension for capturing,
organizing, and verifying web evidence. Captures include MHTML, a full-page screenshot, and
extracted text, sent to the desktop app over `127.0.0.1` and stored locally in per-case
archives. There is no cloud account and no telemetry; captures stay on your machine unless
you export them.

Every capture is fingerprinted with SHA-256, timestamped, and chained to the previous capture
in a per-case manifest. You can verify the chain in-app or export a self-contained HTML report
that a third party can check with nothing more exotic than `jq`, `sha256sum`, and `openssl`.
