The chosen bundle from Sharing Entry Points at handoff fidelity: a Share pill in the top bar that becomes the sync pill (1a, 2b), a ⌘K action beside Join (1e), a “Shared” badge and role line on the dashboard card (3b, 3c), your Member Code on the breadcrumb (3e), a one-time coachmark (4a), and a list of older Cases under the three cards (5b). Then the alternative (1f + 2c) and the 1,000px check. The Members screen is unchanged from Shared Case.dc.html.

1 · One control, four states. The pill in the top bar is always present on a Case. Not shared: “Share”. Invite waiting: “Share · invite waiting”, amber dot. Shared: the shipped sync pill. Intel Mac: “Share” at faint weight; click opens Members, which already carries the unavailable copy. Click always goes to Case settings → Members, so there is one destination to learn.
2 · The approval step stays. Nothing here shares a Case. Every entry opens Members; Invite and Approve are the only actions that change sharing state, and they stay on that screen.
3 · The chip is you. Your own Member Code leads the Case name in the top bar of a shared Case, in every section. A local Case has no chip. Exhibit Numbers (AR-7) then read back to a chip you can see.
4 · Once, then never. The coachmark shows on the first Case opened after this version installs, stored with the tour flags. Clicking the pill, “Show me” or “Got it” all dismiss it for good.

What changes. The breadcrumb opens this menu instead of ⌘K; ⌘K is its first item and the shortcut still works. The Export icon leaves the right cluster, which drops to Connected, health, Settings and theme, so the bar is quieter and there is room to spare at 1,000px. Rename moves here from the dashboard card and the pencil icon.

What it costs. One more click to switch Cases for anyone who learned the breadcrumb. No permanent, visible word “Share” anywhere on the Case; the state of sharing is only the chip and the sync pill, so the coachmark has to point at a closed menu. Export is one level deeper. The label follows 2c: verbs here, “Members” once shared.

Rule. The side groups share what the search leaves. When the right group would push the search under its 128px minimum, the Share pill drops to the 28px icon first; the sync pill, which already has a compact form, follows. At 1,000px with REC running the icon form is needed; without REC the full label fits. Below 900px the Case name truncates as it does today.
